02 / B2C

Nuvama Wealth

Investing, ab sabke liye. A speculative regional campaign making investing feel approachable through familiar North Indian cultural cues.

THE BRIEF

Make investing accessible and culturally relevant for prospective women investors in North India.

THE CREATIVE RESPONSE

Combine familiar food rituals, regional storytelling and financial empowerment.

03 / B2C

Crunch meets calm. A deliberately divisive fusion of Indian snack culture and matcha aesthetics, designed to spark conversation.

THE BRIEF

Win attention in a crowded social feed.

THE CREATIVE RESPONSE

Contrast a sharp, unmistakable crunch with smooth, composed matcha visuals.
