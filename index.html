Trust built through time. An imagined corporate-branding brief about turning an intangible promise into something people can see and feel.

GRANT THORNTON · CONCEPT FILM ▶ PLAY WITH SOUND
Brand trust Corporate film Employer branding

THE BRIEF

Make corporate trust feel human, credible and memorable.

THE INSIGHT

Trust is not declared. It is demonstrated through relationships and consistency.

THE EXECUTION

A restrained cinematic narrative built around time, people and confidence.

02 / B2C

Nuvama Wealth

Investing, ab sabke liye. A speculative campaign exploring how financial communication could become more familiar and approachable for women in Hindi-speaking North India.

NUVAMA WEALTH · CONCEPT FILM ▶ PLAY WITH SOUND
Regional storytelling Financial access Women investors

THE BRIEF

Make investing feel relevant and accessible to an underserved regional audience.

THE INSIGHT

Financial conversations can feel less intimidating when they begin with familiar cultural references.

THE EXECUTION

Everyday North Indian imagery, regional language and an approachable investing narrative.

03 / B2C

Kurkure × Matcha

Crunch meets calm. A deliberately divisive food-fusion concept. Indian snack culture collides with the polished ritual of a Parisian matcha latte.

KURKURE × MATCHA · SPECULATIVE FILM ▶ PLAY WITH SOUND
Rage bait Social-first Food culture

THE BRIEF

Create a scroll-stopping food moment that sparks immediate conversation.

THE INSIGHT

Unexpected cultural combinations can generate curiosity, disagreement and sharing.

THE EXECUTION

Close-up food textures, harsh pouring sounds and a visually unexpected fusion.

04 / B2C

Butter Chicken Lip Gloss

What people think a Punjabi beauty brand would launch. A playful parody of cultural expectations, using an absurd product concept to set up a more surprising beauty-brand story.

BUTTER CHICKEN LIP GLOSS · SPECULATIVE FILM ▶ PLAY WITH SOUND
Beauty Satire Product storytelling

THE BRIEF

Challenge stereotypes around what a Punjabi-inspired beauty brand might sell.

THE INSIGHT

Humour can turn an audience's assumptions into an engaging brand conversation.

THE EXECUTION

A deliberately outrageous lip-gloss concept, followed by a playful brand reveal.
